### 👋 About Me / Hakkımda

I am a **Computer Engineering student at Aydın Adnan Menderes University (ADÜ)**, **Software Core Team Member at GDG on Campus ADÜ**, and a **Software Developer** shipping production products across **Apple App Store**, **Google Play Store**, **macOS**, **Full-Stack Web**, and **Embedded IoT** ecosystems.

- 🎓 **Education & Community:** **Computer Engineering** at ADÜ • Active **Software Core Team Member** at **GDG on Campus ADÜ**.
- 📱 **Published Store Developer:** Official developer on [**Apple App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/ibrahim-temur/id6812348432) & [**Google Play Store**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=%C4%B0brahim+Temur) — creator of **Mac Remote (Desktop Remote)** for iOS, iPadOS, Android & macOS.
- ⚡ **Core Engineering Focus:** Cross-Platform & Native apps (`Flutter`, `SwiftUI`, `Jetpack Compose`, `PyQt6`), Low-Latency Networking (`WebSockets`, `Zeroconf/Bonjour`, `CoreGraphics Quartz`), Full-Stack Web (`React 19`, `ASP.NET Core MVC`, `Node.js`, `PostgreSQL`, `SQL Server`), and Embedded Hardware (`ESP32`, `ESP8266`, `Arduino C/C++`).
- 🌐 **Portfolio & Live Systems:** [**ibrahimtemur.github.io**](https://ibrahimtemur.github.io) • [**GDG Info Session Live**](https://info-session-psi.vercel.app/)
- 📫 **Reach me at:** [**ibrahimtemur847@gmail.com**](mailto:ibrahimtemur847@gmail.com) • [**linkedin.com/in/ibrahim-temur**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ibrahim-temur/)
